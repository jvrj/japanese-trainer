## The campaign (build it exactly like this)
- **1 campaign · ABO · 1 ad set · broad audience** (no interest stacks).
- **Objective/optimization: StartTrial** (the standard event) — NEVER Traffic.
- **1 dynamic-creative ad**: the video + primary texts **H1–H5** + headlines **22 & 24**.
- **Exclusions:** page/IG engagers 365d, site visitors 180d, any customer list.
- **Geo:** US, CA, UK, IE, AU, NZ. Never worldwide.
- **Budget:** $40–50/day for 3–4 days (≈$150 total). Turn ON after midnight.
- Don't peek hourly. Ignore a slow day 1 if days 2–3 come good.

## How to read it (judge at ~1,500 impressions per variant; 1,000 minimum)
| Signal | Pass | Meaning if failed |
|---|---|---|
| Link CTR | ≥ 1% | intros aren't landing — swap intro layer, same body |
| CTR-all : link CTR | ≈ 2.5–3× | ~1:1 = bait clicks (visual sells, copy doesn't) |
| Ranking | judge variants on **CPC**, not CPA | $150 can't read CPA |
| CPM | ~$30 avg; video runs ~30% higher | normal — ignore |
| Clicks → landing views | ≥ 85% | page-speed problem, fix before spending more |

## Kill / iterate rules
- This $150 answers ONE question: **which angle earns the click** (H1–H5). Extract the
  winner's skeleton; every later ad keeps the skeleton, varies the skin.
- Fatigue/iteration = swap **intro + image only** on the proven body. Never reshoot to iterate.
- At real budget later: no conversion by ~3× target CPA in spend → cut.
- **Rejections:** never DELETE a rejected ad. Appeal at most once; else edit to vanilla,
  get approved, turn off, tag DNU. H5 (green owl) is the watch-listed variant.
- Winning intro phrases get promoted into the landing headline + emails (intros ARE research).
