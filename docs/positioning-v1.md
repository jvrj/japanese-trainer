# Isshin — Positioning v1 (locked 2026-07-31)

Owner-approved go-to-market message. The landing page and every ad build from this
doc. It adapts via ad A/B results — not via pre-launch rewrites.

## Headline

**Speak Japanese from day one — with an AI friend who never judges you.**

## Positioning statement

Isshin is a Japanese-only speaking partner. No lessons to click through, no
flashcard grind, no waiting until you're "ready" — you open it, talk, and it
talks back. It understands you even when you get it wrong, corrects you kindly,
and never makes you feel stupid. Built for people who can read kana but freeze
when it's time to actually speak.

## Three proof points (landing-page bullets / ad variants)

1. **Hands-free conversation** — speak, it hears you, it answers. Like talking
   to a patient friend, not operating an app.
2. **Actually responds to what YOU say** — not a scripted lesson that drags you
   back on-topic. (Praktika's most-cited flaw, inverted; never name them.)
3. **Fair deal** — free trial, no card required, simple monthly price, cancel
   anytime. (Category's #1 churn driver, inverted into the trust pitch.)

## Price

$8.99 USD/mo. Undercuts Langua (~$12.50/mo annual) and Praktika (~$8/mo but
3-month lock-in); inside the proven $8.99–14.99 category band. Free trial, no
card.

## Ad format (first campaign)

20–30s screen recording of a REAL session: imperfect Japanese spoken → Isshin
understands, corrects kindly → conversation keeps flowing. The
"mistake handled kindly" moment is the ad (the format behind Praktika's viral
influencer growth). Run 2–3 headline variants; let clicks pick the winner.

With presence on (Delve 10 — contingent on its Gate 0 owner approval), the
recording shows the partner's illustrated face center-stage — the thumbnail is
the "AI friend", face and name, never a staged shot; generator licensing must be
verified before any paid placement.

## Rules

- Every claim above must stay TRUE of the shipped app (the ad-vs-reality gap is
  the category's most-punished sin — Trustpilot 3.7 vs app-store 4.8).
- Secondary asset (retention, not acquisition): the vocab/drill deck (~1700
  words, SRS) — "and it builds your vocabulary while you talk."
- ICP: adult self-learner, knows kana + some vocab, cannot yet hold a spoken
  sentence. (The owner is the reference user.)
