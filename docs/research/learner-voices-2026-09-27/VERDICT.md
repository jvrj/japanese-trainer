# Is the learner-voices research true? Verdict (27 Sep 2026)

We ran three independent checks on the research:
1. **Recount** (`verify-1-recount.md`): rebuilt every number from the raw review files.
2. **Why it would be true** (`verify-2-why-true.md`): studies, regulator data and company filings.
3. **Why it may be false** (`verify-3-why-false.md`): what people actually pay for and use.

**Rule of thumb:** comments show what people *say*. Only the ad test shows what they *do*. Nothing below is "certain" until money moves.

## Near-certain (held in all three checks)

| Finding | Why |
|---|---|
| Saying the word from memory makes it stick | Lab studies: about 80% vs 33% recall a week later. |
| "It picks for me" beats choosing | Duolingo removed lesson choice in 2022, then had its best quarter to date. |
| The Japan trip is a real, growing market | 42.7M visitors to Japan in 2025; US visitors up 21%. |
| Pimsleur teaches few words per lesson | Its own FAQ: 10–17 per 30-minute lesson. |
| Anger at subscriptions is about the process, not the price | Only 6% of angry reviews are about price. The rest are about refunds, cancelling, surprise charges and no reminder. Australia: 76% had trouble cancelling. |
| Being misheard by the mic makes people angry | Speech recognisers make twice the errors on some accents. The Teuida complaints recount at 9–10% of its reviews. |
| Show the written word with the audio | Consistent in the research. Use kana, not romaji. |

## Likely, but narrower than we said

| Finding | Correction |
|---|---|
| "Years of apps, still can't speak" | Real, but our searches used those exact words, so it came back loud. In the stores it's only 54 of 92,878 reviews. Giving up (motivation) was mentioned more. Duolingo keeps growing (12.7M payers), so it's a pain for existing app users, not proof a stranger will pay. |
| Card trial is fine | Trials that need a card convert far better (about 49% vs 18%). But no public number covers a cold Facebook click typing a card into our own checkout page. |
| Older learners | They learn fine when they set the pace. No evidence they feel ignored. |
| Travellers learn in the car | 262 happy reviews mention driving or the commute. But recalling and speaking is closer to a phone call than to the radio, and NSW learner/P1 drivers can't use a phone at all. |

## Wrong, or reversed

| We said | Truth |
|---|---|
| Duolingo hate = silly sentences, not hearts | Duolingo's own low-star reviews: hearts/energy 185, silly sentences 22, can't speak 5. **Hearts IS the top complaint.** |
| "It never marks your accent" is an ad angle | People want grading that *works*, not no grading. ELSA (34M users) is built on scoring. Keep the mic out of grading, but don't sell it. |
| Don't say AI; AI partners are split | App reviews run 53 positive to 34 negative on AI partners. Speak earns about $100M a year. The backlash is against AI-*made content* and AI replacing staff. |
| Price lines $8–10/mo, $40/yr, $80 "won't pay" | Built on 1–5 reviews each. Not evidence. |
| 456 Babbel users = ready paying audience | The count is right, but only 5 mention a trip and 14 mention paying. |
| Pimsleur's weakness = too few words | People say it, but pay for "speaking in 30 days". It's still the most-liked app with our buyer. |
| "Hazard/illegal" driving complaints | About 2 reviews, not a theme. |

## What went wrong in how we researched

1. **Searches were worded like the claims**, so the claims came back.
2. **Counts had no totals**, and Babbel is 44% of the store sample.
3. **Raw TikTok and Facebook text wasn't saved**, and one thread was 49% of the Facebook comments. Use those quotes for wording, not for "how common".

## What only the $300 test can settle

- Three ads, identical except the message, at least $100 each:
  1. **A promise with a time limit** (for example "Order dinner in Japanese before your trip"). Rivals' long-running copy is always this shape.
  2. **The pain line** ("110-day streak and I can't say a thing").
  3. **Hands-free** (optional).
- Pick the winner by click rate and cost per landing-page view.
- Track landing → plans → card entered. If fewer than about 1 in 10 who reach plans enter a card, **the card is the problem, not the ad**.
- Set Meta to optimise for checkout-start. $300 buys too few trials to judge trial-to-paid.
- Write the kill lines down before launch.
