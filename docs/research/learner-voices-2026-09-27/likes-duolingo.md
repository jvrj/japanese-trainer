# What learners LIKE about Duolingo (Japanese focus), in their own words

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. Group: Duolingo (Japanese course, Max / Video Call / Roleplay, streaks, leagues, XP, owl, notifications, Super pricing, path, stories, audio lessons, family plan).

**How it was gathered**
- **Google Play:** 2,477 English reviews pulled with the `google-play-scraper` library. 1,442 are 4 or 5 stars. 129 mention Japanese, hiragana, katakana or kanji.
- **Apple App Store:** 450 reviews from the public RSS feed, across the US, UK, Australia and Canada. 302 are 4 or 5 stars.
- **Hacker News:** comments found through the Algolia API.
- **YouTube:** comments from 9 review videos, downloaded with `yt-dlp`. 4 of them had useful praise.
- **Blogs, reviews and forums:** about 25 pages, fetched directly.
- **Reddit:** not fetched, and I did not try to get past any bot check. Search snippets that quote Reddit barely showed up, so Reddit is under-represented. The one Reddit-attributed line (from JLPT Samurai) is marked as coming second-hand.

**How to read the counts:** "Web sources" counts distinct pages or comments outside the two app stores. "Play keyword hits" counts how many of the 1,442 positive Play reviews contain the theme's keywords. That is a rough measure: the words can appear in mixed reviews.

**Store URLs used below**
- PS = https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.duolingo (reviews pulled Sep 2026)
- AS = https://apps.apple.com/us/app/duolingo-language-lessons/id570060128 (RSS reviews, US/GB/AU/CA)

## 2. Why they pay
1. **To remove the limits on hearts or energy.** This is the biggest reason by far.
   - "now that I've bought super I can do as many as a like" (AS, GB)
   - "unlimited attempts to complete a lesson without worry" (https://hardlyhamilton.com/2023/05/02/super-duolingo-worth-money/)
   - "super takes all the annoyance out of really diving into" a course (PS)
2. **To get rid of ads.**
   - "Dropping the Ads alone is totally worth the subscription if you're a daily user" (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35155256)
   - "ad-free experience has really changed the game" (hardlyhamilton, above)
3. **The family plan.** One payer covers kids or relatives.
   - "I opted in to the family plan… kids are learning… Japanese" (PS)
   - "lucky my nan got the family plan" (AS, GB)
4. **Lily Video Call (Max).** "TOTALLY WORTH THE MONEY" (Threads). Another subscriber upgraded "when an offer popped up" (https://theowlandme.blog/2026/01/10/review-duolingo-max-video-calls/).
5. **Seeing results from a practice that lasts.** "given Duolingo money for two consecutive years… I have been seeing results" (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35672287)
6. **A trip coming up.** "I recommend getting super if you are going to a new country" (AS, CA). Another: "going to japan… this really helped" (AS, CA)
7. **The free trial converts people.** "very much worth it for the 1 month free trial" (AS, CA). Another: "Opt for Super, the free version is way too limited." (AS, CA)
8. **Paying as a commitment device.** HardlyHamilton frames the subscription as a "financial motivator" for building the habit. That is the author's framing, not a direct quote.
9. **Price is the main objection even among fans.** "please make super Duolingo and Duolingo max less expensive" (AS, GB). "Really wish they'd add a Lifetime Plan" (PS).

## 3. Why they stay or come back
- **Fear of losing the streak.** "I don't want to lose my 800 day streak" (PS). Another: "The streak is actually the only thing I really like about it" (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16824954). The streak also works as a visible record of time spent: "it shows me how long I've been working on it" (same HN comment).
- **A fixed slot in the day.** "I have it set up to need to do a daily lesson before I open any social media app" (AS, US). Another: "encourages practice with xp and app blocking is brilliant" (AS, GB)
- **They look forward to it.** "look forward to it each day" (PS). Another: "I genuinely look forward to learning every day!" (Japanese learner, PS)
- **Small wins stack up.** "I can see my progress every day" (Japanese learner, PS). Another: "casually it approaches you to make consistent efforts every day, without overexerting" (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22159018)
- **Comfort and routine.** A Japanese learner going "through a bit of a rough patch" says it "has really helped me maintain my sanity" (AS, GB)
- **Doing it with other people.** A sister ritual, friend streaks, a family plan (see theme 9). Friend Streaks are "a great concept" for "beginners who need extra motivation" (https://duoplanet.com/duolingo-friend-streaks/)
- **The owl's guilt trip.** "doing a great job at making you feel guilty for not visiting" (PS). Another: "the owl motivates you to come everyday" (AS, GB)
- **Gratitude despite complaints.** "There's a lot of things about Duolingo I don't love but I'm incredibly grateful that it exists." (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44101022)
- **What makes people leave.** Losing the streak and its feelings with it: "I used to love it", followed by the $38 ask to restore it (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22162289). And courses reordered under them, energy caps, and removed features (audio lessons, the forum).

## 5. What WordStick could borrow (one line each, honestly)
- **A streak as a visible record of days showed up, with a forgiving freeze.** The loss-aversion stick is what makes people quit when it breaks.
- **Make the first win "I can read/say it after N days".** The "17 days… I can finally read Japanese" moment is the kind of line that gets shared.
- **Voice turns that feel safe.** "No anxiety" and "no teacher watching" are liked for their own sake, and they fit a car or a train.
- **Hands-free audio is a proven love that Duolingo removed.** "Best feature on this app… I will come back to Super" is an opening WordStick can own.
- **A tiny sound and a verbal "nice one" after each correct recall.** The ding and the praise get mentioned unprompted.
- **One character with memory and a bit of attitude.** "It's you again?" delights people; a mascot is optional, personality is not.
- **A real-world payoff prompt.** Ask "Did you use a word today?" to surface the temple-kanji or complimented-accent moments.
- **A family or friend plan and a shared daily ritual.** "Our daily thing" with a sister is a stay-reason, not just a pricing tier.
- **A habit anchor.** "Before I open social media" suggests "before you start the car / first stop on the train" as a setup step.
- **The paid tier removes friction, not learning.** People pay to stop being capped, never to unlock the basics. A capped free tier breeds resentment in reviews.
- **Ship a free trial, but show the price honestly.** Fans still say "please make it less expensive" and ask for a lifetime plan.
- **Never reorder a learner's progress under them.** Course reshuffles are the most common reason Japanese learners' love turns sour.
