# Facebook groups and TikTok: learner voices (harvested 27 Sep 2026)

**How it was gathered**
- **Facebook:** read in the owner's logged-in Chrome. The posts come from public groups, found by searching posts for "pimsleur japanese", "learning japanese before trip app", "learning japanese my wife husband app" and "duolingo japanese can't speak". Nearly every useful thread was in **Japan Travel Tips & Planning**, a public group of mostly 35+ travellers. One thread came from a Tokyo Disney holiday-planning group.
  - 10 threads were read in full, about 500 comments in all, plus 6 more posts seen on search cards.
  - Nothing was posted, liked or joined.
  - Names are left out on purpose; links go to the thread.
- **TikTok:** your Chrome isn't logged into TikTok, so no login was used. I read TikTok's public comment feed for **118 videos** found on 35 of TikTok's own public topic pages (for example "what are some good apps to learn japanese", "using pimsleur", "japanese duolingo"). That gave **3,109 comments** of 25+ characters from 97 videos. They were keyword-sorted, then read by hand from the most-liked down.
- Every quote is word for word and under 15 words. "Likes" is the number of likes on that one comment.
- **What was lost:** one batch of Facebook threads was wiped by Facebook's own page storage partway through and had to be re-read. Facebook's search only shows a handful of posts per search, and each extra search mostly turned up ads and creator promos, so I stopped at 10 threads.

**Who these people are**
- **Facebook** is our buyer: 35+ adults with a trip date (March '27, Oct '27, "19 months"), spouses, a dad who runs a business, a woman born in Japan who is now a grandmother. This is the audience the Meta ads will reach.
- **TikTok** is younger, anime-adjacent and expects things free ("Is it paid..? bc I'm poor", 74 likes). It is **not our buyer**, but it's useful as a contrast.

## New things that showed up in BOTH places, or on Facebook and in another round

1. **The fear is the reply, not the question.** This came up on Facebook three times, from travellers, and on the Rick Steves forum.
   - "Understanding the answer is something else entirely." (a former language teacher)
   - "I always worry about asking a question and getting a response in Japanese and can't respond!"
   - "the biggest issue... struggling to understand what other people are saying back to you"
   - "when these live humans go off script"
2. **Audio-only needs the written word.** This came from TikTok, with big likes, and from the travellers.
   - "hard to tell the proper pronunciation sometimes because nothing is written" (1,822 likes)
   - "recommend checking quizlet to supplement the lack of literacy" (2,064 likes)
   - "I can't remember a word unless I see it written out"
3. **Speech grading makes people angry.**
   - "I'M STUCK ON DAY 9 PRACTICE QUIZ BECAUSE OF MY ACCENT" (699 likes, TikTok)
   - This matches the store round, where Teuida has 416 such complaints. WordStick's microphone never grades, and that is a selling point we don't use yet.
4. **Being thanked by locals for trying is the reward.**
   - "Japanese people are genuinely grateful and delighted when you speak to them in Japanese" (post with 303 likes)
   - "The cast members I interacted with were so appreciative whenever I'd try"
   - "many Japanese people complimented me on my very poor attempts"
5. **Pimsleur feels slow, and people want more words.**
   - "it all seems like baby talk"
   - "I did the whole Korean one... (about 10 words a lesson)"
   - "wish I could have finished the whole thing before going... just not enough time!"
   - A trip deadline plus a slow course is the gap WordStick fills.
6. **Active beats passive.** "listening to Pimsleur you can drift away at times and not learn." This is praise for an app that "forces you to interact". It is our drill's whole design, in a buyer's words.
7. **Heritage learners carry real emotion.**
   - "Now that my mom has passed, I'm now the one in the family to carry on"
   - "childhood books in Hiragana I want to be able to read to my grandchildren"
8. **Google Translate is the rival for "how much do I need?"**
   - "about 100 words and phrases - backed up with google translate"
   - Translator apps get recommended over learning.
   - What survives that rival is the *warmth* of trying (point 4).
