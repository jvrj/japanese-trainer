# What learners LIKE about AI speaking apps and human tutors

Research date: 27 Sep 2026. Group: Praktika, Speak, ChatGPT Advanced Voice, Langua (LanguaTalk), Loora, TalkPal, Jumpspeak, Univerbal, Gliglish, Duolingo Video Call, italki, Preply, HelloTalk, Tandem.

**How the quotes were gathered (read this first):**
- YouTube comments came straight from yt-dlp (13 videos, about 280 comments) and are copied word for word.
- Store, Trustpilot and Product Hunt quotes came through a page-reading step that pulls short excerpts. They are real review text, but a few may have been lightly trimmed. Anything that was clearly the company's own marketing text has been left out.
- Reddit was not fetched, because it blocks automated readers with a bot check. No search result quoted Reddit in a usable way, so there are **no Reddit quotes** here.
- Japanese-specific praise is thin in public reviews. Most reviews are for Spanish, French, German or English. Anything about Japanese is marked **[JP]**.
- "Count" means the number of *distinct sources* where the theme came up, not the number of quotes.

## 2. Why they pay
1. **It replaces a tutor they couldn't afford or schedule.** This is the most common framing: "don't have to pay 50 euros", "negligible compared to tutors", and $30/month against $15–20/hour (HN user jameshush, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42772885).
2. **They can feel progress in speaking within weeks.**
   - "After only a few weeks I already feel my conversation skills have improved" (Langua, reviews.io)
   - "I have seen a drastic improvement in my speaking ability" (TalkPal, App Store)
3. **The feedback is something plain ChatGPT won't give.** Paying users say the correction layer is what separates a paid app from a free chatbot. ChatGPT users complain it praises wrong answers.
4. **Low-risk entry helps people commit.**
   - "it's good they have a 30 day money back guarantee" (Langua)
   - italki's "$5 trials, no subscription"
5. **A person they've found and don't want to lose (human tutors).** "if I had the money, I'd book him every day!" and "3 community tutors each week" (italki video).

## 3. Why they stay or come back
1. **They look forward to the conversation itself.**
   - "I actually want to talk more now that I have engaged with Lilly" (Duolingo, about 6 weeks in)
   - "keeps me wanting to comeback for more" (Speak)
2. **The characters hook them.** "Their avatars keep me hooked" (Praktika).
3. **They see daily progress.**
   - "I feel that I am making progress daily" (Praktika Trustpilot p.2)
   - A 209-day Speak streak kept going because review was handled automatically (Lingtuitive)
4. **It feels like companionship.** "It was like having a personal teacher to monitor and correct... I looked forward to it" (55 likes, a lonely senior who paused because it felt addictive): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=czyWvwEW_g0
5. **It remembers them.** Langua remembering facts "months down the road" is named as a reason for a 2-year subscription.
6. **Relationships (human and exchange apps).** Friendships on HelloTalk and Tandem, and a tutor who "adapts each class to what I actually need" (italki TP).

## 5. What WordStick and Talk could borrow (one line each)
1. **Make the no-judgment line concrete in the copy** ("take as long as you like, nobody's waiting"). It is the #2 theme and fits hands-free, out-loud drilling well.
2. **Talk must never cut in when you pause.** This is the #1 ChatGPT voice complaint. A patient end-of-turn is part of the value, not a technical detail.
3. **Correct by echoing: "you said X, natives say Y".** This is the most praised correction style. It works in voice, and STT can stay a non-grader here because Talk models the line rather than scoring it.
4. **Memory is the moat.** "Remembers the words you own" sits right on the gap reviewers call out in ChatGPT ("every conversation starts fresh"), so say it plainly in marketing.
5. **Words from Talk should flow back into the drill, and the reverse.** Langua's "save mid-chat, woven back into conversations" is exactly this loop, and users praise it.
6. **Give Talk one warm, specific character** (Lily's dog, Sky's gestures). A light personality drives retention, but keep it optional, since avatars also get called "uncanny" or "stupid".
7. **Offer a slow-down control and time to think.** This was asked for over and over ("I need one where they speak veerrrry slowly").
8. **Pricing honesty is a competitive weapon.** Hidden prices and hard cancellation are the loudest anger in this group (105 and 36 likes). Show the price up front and make cancelling easy.
9. **Frame the value against a tutor, not against Duolingo** ("a speaking partner for less than one tutor lesson a month"). That is how payers justify it to themselves.
10. **Japanese-specific quality is a real gap.** Reviews say TalkPal "drops to a basic chatbot" for Japanese and ChatGPT's Japanese accent is "far from native". A native-sounding Japanese voice is a real differentiator, but only if it's true.
11. **Hands-free chores and walks are a named use case.** Use it in ad scenes (dishes, walking), in line with the circumstance-first copy.
12. **Don't claim the AI replaces warmth.** Learners still prize a human who "EXPLAINS" the grammar. Talk's explanations should say *why*, not just give the answer.
