## Overview

This report enumerates CLOSED slot sets in beginner Japanese (N5–N4) with exact membership, citing primary sources. A **closed set** is a finishable inventory (e.g., position words, polite verb endings) that a learner can complete; an **open set** is unbounded vocabulary (e.g., nouns, all adjectives). The learner is attending a formal Japanese class while building an AI drill app, so classroom order and textbook alignment are critical.

## 3. DEMONSTRATIVES (KOSOADO SYSTEM)

**Source:** Nihongo Know Complete Guide; Genki I Chapters 1–2  
**JLPT Level:** N5  
**System Structure:** Four distances (ko/so/a/do) × Seven word types

### Complete Kosoado Forms

**Pronouns:** これ (this) | それ (that) | あれ (that over there) | どれ (which)

**Adjectives:** この | その | あの | どの

**Places:** ここ (here) | そこ (there) | あそこ (over there) | どこ (where)

**Directions (Polite):** こちら | そちら | あちら | どちら

**Directions (Casual):** こっち | そっち | あっち | どっち

**Manner:** こう (like this) | そう (like that) | ああ (like that) | どう (how)

**Kind/Type:** こんな | そんな | あんな | どんな

**Amount:** このくらい | そのくらい | あのくらい | どのくらい

**Total:** 28 core forms  
**Finishability:** HIGH. All taught as a paradigm; visual distance aids retention.

## 9. THE ADVICE FRAME: V-た hou ga ii (ほうがいい)

**Source:** CotoAcademy; JLPTSensei.com; Japanese Ammo  
**JLPT Level:** N5  
**Classroom Location:** Genki I Chapter 12

### Structure & Forms

| Form | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Affirmative | 行った ほうがいい (itta hou ga ii) | You should go |
| Negative | 行かない ほうがいい (ikanai hou ga ii) | You shouldn't go |

### [LENS] CRITICAL: Relationship to Comparison Frame "A yori B no hou ga"

Both use the word ほう (方 = "way") but serve different functions:

| Frame | Structure | Function | JLPT |
|---|---|---|---|
| V-ta hou ga ii | V-ta + ほうがいい | Advice: "you should do X" | N5 |
| A yori B no hou ga | A より B の ほうが + Adjective | Comparison: "B is more [adj] than A" | N5 |

**Key Finding:** Both are N5, taught 2 chapters apart in Genki (Ch.10 vs. Ch.12). They share the root word hou but differ in grammar and function. Sources teach them separately; no conflation found.

**Nuance:** ～ほうがいい conveys stronger recommendation than softer alternatives (～ばいい, ～たらいい).

**Finishability:** HIGH for the frame. Open slot is the verb; the advice-giving structure is closed.

## 10. GENKI I INTRODUCTION ORDER (Lessons 1–12)

**Source:** St. Olaf Genki Grammar Index

Genki I introduces patterns chapter-by-chapter:

- **Ch.1:** Question sentences, desu, possessive の
- **Ch.2:** Demonstratives (kosoado system foundation)
- **Ch.3:** Present tense verbs, particles (は)
- **Ch.4:** ある/いる, past tense, **location words**, duration
- **Ch.5:** Adjectives, counting, suki/kirai, volitional masho
- **Ch.6:** Two activities, -kara (reason), -tekudasai (request), permission
- **Ch.7:** Relative descriptions, -ni iku (purpose), -teiru (ongoing)
- **Ch.8:** Particle が, short forms, nanika/nanimo
- **Ch.9:** Relative clauses (qualifying nouns), past short form, mada
- **Ch.10:** **Comparison (yori, no hou ga)**, particle de (location/reason)
- **Ch.11:** Listing (ya), habitual (-kotoga-aru), desire (-tai)
- **Ch.12:** **hou ga ii (advice)**, -desho (conjecture), -n-desu (explanation), -node, obligation, excess
