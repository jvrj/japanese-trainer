Why saying it works better than seeing it

Every rep follows the same shape: hear the cue, pause, say the answer yourself, then hear the real thing. That pause where you have to reach for the word — not just recognise it — is what makes it stick. Underneath, a scheduler quietly resurfaces each word right before you'd forget it, so the words from Monday's drive show up again exactly when Wednesday's drive needs them.

The research behind it: two of the best-replicated findings in memory science — retrieving an answer from memory beats re-reading it (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006), and spreading reviews out beats cramming (Cepeda et al., 2006). That's the entire mechanism — pointed at your commute.

Questions

Can I really do this without looking at my phone?

Yes, for the core drill. It speaks, pauses, reveals the answer, and continues on its own. Glance down for spelling or a hint whenever you like — the loop never requires it.

Is my voice recorded or stored anywhere?

No — nothing you say is ever recorded or kept. Speech is handled by your phone's own voice services, and answering by voice is optional anyway: tapping or typing works exactly the same. Details in our privacy policy.

What if I miss a few days — or a few weeks?

Nothing bad. There's no queue to catch up on and no pile of red cards waiting. A streak exists, if you switch it on. Nothing else is watching. You open the app, it picks up, you continue.

What phones does it work on?

It runs in the browser — best on Android with Chrome today, fine on most modern phones. Speech recognition is only a trigger to move the drill along, never a grade — if the mic misses you (car noise, cheap earbuds), tap or type instead. Nothing about your progress depends on being heard perfectly.

Isn't there a free option already, like Language Transfer?

Yes — Language Transfer is genuinely free and genuinely good. Isshin's difference is hands-free active recall with spaced repetition over a ~1,700-word deck rather than a fixed lesson track — and right now Isshin is free too, so try both.
