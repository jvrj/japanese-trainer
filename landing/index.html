Made for the time you already have

Flashcard apps need your eyes and your thumbs. Isshin only needs your ears and your voice — so your commute, your walk, and your chores become study time.

🎧

Truly hands-free

The drill runs itself: it speaks, waits while you think, reveals the answer, and moves on. Phone in your pocket, hands on the wheel or in the dishwater. Prefer tapping or typing? Both work too.

🗣

You answer out loud

Saying the word yourself — not recognising it on a card — is what moves it into long-term memory. Every step in Isshin is active recall: hear the cue, produce the answer, then hear the real thing.

👂

Listening first, speaking second

Brand-new words arrive as listening steps — Isshin says the Japanese, you say what it means. Once a word settles in, it flips: you hear English and produce the Japanese. One tap flips the whole drill's direction whenever you like.

🧠

Spaced repetition that trusts you

A proven scheduler brings each word back just before you'd forget it. And instead of a robot grading your accent, Isshin simply asks you how it went — 😬 🙂 💪 — and schedules accordingly. No red X's, no grades, ever.

🎌

Japanese only, from zero

Not 40 languages wide — one language deep. A curated ~1,700-word core deck, everything in kana with audio (the app teaches you kana too), plus sentence drills, verb forms, and counting.

🔒

Nothing leaves your phone

Speech happens through your phone's built-in voice service — your audio is never sent to, stored on, or heard by our servers. No ad trackers, no data selling. It even works offline once loaded.

Why out loud, why spaced? The two most robust findings in memory research: actively retrieving an answer beats re-reading it (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006), and spacing reviews out beats cramming (Cepeda et al., 2006). Isshin is those two findings, pointed at Japanese, with your eyes closed.

Questions

I can't read Japanese at all. Can I start?

Yes — Isshin starts from zero. Everything is written in kana (the phonetic script) with audio on every word, and the app teaches you kana itself. No kanji is thrown at you.

Do I really not need to look at the screen?

For the core drill, no. It speaks the cue, leaves you a thinking gap, then speaks the answer and moves on by itself. Glance at the screen whenever you want the spelling or a hint — but the loop never requires it.

Is my voice recorded or stored?

No. Speech recognition happens through your phone's built-in service; Isshin's servers never receive, store, or hear your audio. Details in our privacy policy.

What phones does it work on?

It runs in the browser — best on Android with Chrome today, and fine on most modern phones. The speak-aloud features use your device's own speech engine; if a phone lacks one, you can still tap or type your answers.

What happens when early access ends?

The price above goes live with a 7-day free trial, and we'll say so clearly before anything changes. Your progress is stored on your device and stays with you either way.
